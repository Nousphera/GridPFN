Method details

Each dot is one household’s average. Mean ± sample SD describes differences between homes, not a confidence interval or variation across seeds. Comparisons subtract the other method from TabPFN. Percent differences use the other method’s positive mean; zero or negative baselines use an absolute difference. Comfort uses percentage points.

Each test month follows a separate prior-week selection and a refit on all earlier training and validation days. All final checkpoints are frozen before test scoring. Earlier months become historical data for later folds; October trains no reported model.

One seed, the same homes, and June–October observations. These results do not establish winter performance, generalization to unseen homes, real-world savings, or a globally optimal learned policy. TabFM weights retain their research-only license.
